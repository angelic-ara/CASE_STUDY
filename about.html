Our Mission

We created Gipit Eats to help people find easy, delicious, and affordable recipes. We want to show that cooking good food doesn't have to be expensive. Through our website, we hope to help people save money, learn simple cooking skills, and share their own recipes with others. We believe that even on a tight budget, everyone can enjoy good food!

About the Authors

Hi! We're the creators behind Gipit Eats, a pair of student food lovers who believe that delicious meals don't have to be expensive. As students, we understand the struggle of balancing schoolwork, a limited allowance, and sudden food cravings. We created Gipit Eats to share simple, affordable, and student-friendly recipes that anyone can try. Through this project, we hope to inspire fellow students to explore cooking, plan their meals, and make the most of their budget. Because kahit gipit, deserve pa rin ang masarap na pagkain!
