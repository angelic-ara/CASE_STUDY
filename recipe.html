Affordable & Quick Meals for College Students

🌭 1. Fried Hotdog with Garlic Rice

Prep Time: 5-8 mins | Approx. Cost: ₱35 - ₱40

Ingredients:

  • 2 pieces Hotdog (notched/sliced diagonally)
  • 1 cup Cooked Rice (preferably cold/leftover)
  • 1 clove Garlic (minced)
  • 1 tbsp Cooking Oil
  • Ketchup or Mayo (optional topping)

Cooking Steps:

  1. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Fry the hotdogs for 3 to 4 minutes until fully cooked and slightly browned. Set aside.
  3. In the same pan, saute minced garlic until golden brown.
  4. Add the cooked rice, break any clumps, and mix well with the garlic for 2 minutes.
  5. Serve hotdog over garlic rice with your choice of sauce.

🐟 2. Century Tuna & Calamansi Combo

Prep Time: 5 mins | Approx. Cost: ₱40 - ₱45

Ingredients:

  • 1 can Century Tuna (Flakes in Oil or Hot & Spicy)
  • 1 small Onion (chopped)
  • 1 clove Garlic (minced)
  • 1 cup Warm Cooked Rice
  • Squeeze of Calamansi or Soy Sauce (optional)

Cooking Steps:

  1. Saute garlic and chopped onion in a pan until soft and fragrant.
  2. Pour in the entire can of Century Tuna (including the oil for flavor).
  3. Stir-fry for 2 to 3 minutes until warm and well combined.
  4. (Optional) Add a squeeze of calamansi or a dash of soy sauce for extra zest.
  5. Serve hot on top of a fresh bowl of rice.

🥩 3. Gisadong Corned Beef with Potatoes

Prep Time: 8-10 mins | Approx. Cost: ₱45 - ₱55

Ingredients:

  • 1 small can Corned Beef
  • 1 small Potato (cut into small cubes)
  • 1 small Onion (sliced)
  • 1 tbsp Cooking Oil
  • 1 cup Cooked Rice

Cooking Steps:

  1. Heat oil in a pan and fry potato cubes until golden and crisp. Set aside.
  2. Saute sliced onions until translucent.
  3. Add the corned beef and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until hot.
  4. Mix the fried potato cubes back into the corned beef.
  5. Serve steaming hot alongside warm rice.

🍜 4. Pancit Canton & Boiled Egg Upgrade

Prep Time: 5 mins | Approx. Cost: ₱30 - ₱45

Ingredients:

  • 1-2 packs Pancit Canton (Any flavor)
  • 1 Egg (Boiled or fried)
  • Optional: Toasted bread or calamansi

Cooking Steps:

  1. Boil noodles until cooked, then drain water.
  2. Mix the seasoning and oil packets thoroughly.
  3. Top with a soft-boiled or sunny-side-up egg and serve hot.
